Urban Spoken English · How to teach

How to teach

Every lesson is 60 minutes: warm-up 8, new language 10, guided practice 12, free speaking 18, polish 7, homework 5. After the first ten minutes, students should speak more than the teacher.

Correct the day’s target sentences during guided practice. Do not stop a story for small grammar. Never mock a student. Never treat adult beginners like children. Mother-tongue instructions are allowed at Beginner; the speech you want back is English.

Homework is a voice note or a real line in the city. Reply with one glow and one grow.

Urban Spoken English · Placement

Place a student

Age first, then a 10-minute talk: name, family, a normal day, last Sunday, next weekend, a photo, an opinion. For older students, a team story or why this job.

Silence or one word → Beginner. Short present sentences → Elementary / Everyday. Understands but freezes → Fluency. Can argue and wants polish → Upper or Interview. When in doubt, start easier.

Timelines

6-week starter: two hours a week, only lessons tagged 6-week (the spine). 3-month core: lessons tagged 3-month. 6-month: every lesson. Crash: daily, only crash-tagged lessons, for Interview and teen orals.

Children · First words that work

Children 6–10 Absolute Beginner / Beginner 48 lessons

Names, family, tiffin, park, shop, and school. Games first. Full sentences soon.

Who: Urban children aged 6–10 who are new to speaking English, including those who can read a little from school.

Done when: The child can greet, name family and school things, buy one item, ask for help, and give a one-minute ‘All about me’.

Place here when: Place here if the child answers with one word or silence, and needs pictures and movement.

Teacher note: Keep lessons physical. Sit on the floor if that helps. Never make a child perform before they have rehearsed with a partner.

Tests

StartName, hello, point to three colours or objects.
Mid (after lesson 24)Name, age, family, one polite request, favourite food.
EndOne-minute talk plus shop or help role-play.

Lessons

Lesson 01 · Hello, my name is 6w, 3m, 6m

Greet the teacher and say your name in a full sentence.

Say this: Hello. My name is Aanya. · What is your name? · Nice to meet you.

Warm-up. Stand in a circle. Wave and say Hello. Teacher says Hello, I am ___ and walks to two children. Teach. Hold up a name-card. Model: My name is ___. Children repeat. Show What is your name? as a question face. Guided. Ball toss: catch, say My name is ___, ask the next child What is your name? Free. Walk-and-meet: children shake hands with three classmates and use all three sentences. Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.

Homework. WhatsApp voice note: Hello. My name is ___. Nice to meet you.

Slips. I name is Rohan → My name is Rohan; What your name? → What is your name?

Lesson 02 · How are you? 6w, 3m, 6m

Ask and answer How are you? with happy, okay, and tired.

Say this: How are you? · I am fine, thank you. · I am tired. · I am happy.

Warm-up. Face cards: happy, okay, tired. Children copy the face. Teach. Teacher asks How are you? and points to a face. Class answers together. Guided. Two lines face each other. Ask and answer, then one line steps right. Free. Doctor-clinic role-play: one child is the teacher at the door, others enter and answer. Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.

Homework. Ask one family member How are you? and report their answer tomorrow.

Slips. I fine → I am fine; I am fine thank you you? → I am fine, thank you.

Lesson 03 · Numbers 1 to 10 6w, 3m, 6m

Count objects and say how many.

Say this: How many? · There are seven. · I have three pencils.

Warm-up. Clap-count to 10. Whisper-count back down. Teach. Show fingers, then classroom objects. Model There are ___. Guided. Count pencils, books, water bottles in pairs. Teacher checks numbers. Free. Shop tray: put 1–10 erasers. Partner asks How many? Other answers. Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.

Homework. Count five things at home and say There are ___.

Slips. They are seven → There are seven; I have two pencil → I have two pencils
