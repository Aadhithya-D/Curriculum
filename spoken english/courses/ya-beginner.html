Young adults · Campus English from the ground

College, commute, money, hostel, and a human talk about this year.

Young adults 18–25Beginner31 lessons24–72 hours depending on timeline

Who this is for

Urban young adults starting spoken English seriously — college, first job, or a gap year.

They are done when: They can move around campus in English, split a bill, call a classmate, and give a two-minute year talk.

Place a student here when: Place here if they still need full-sentence building on adult topics.

Keep the register adult. Money and stress belong in this course. No children’s games unless they ask.

01

A grown-up hello

Introduce yourself as a student or first-job person, not a schoolchild.

6w3m6m

Say this today

I’m Rohan. I’m in my second year of B.Com.I live in Andheri.I’m here to speak more confidently.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Name, course or job, area of the city, why this class.
8–18 min
New language. Ban Myself Rohan. Teach I’m / I live in / I’m here to...
18–30 min
Guided practice. Meet three people. Remember one fact each.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Introduce a partner to the group.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. 45-second intro voice note.

Common slips

Not this: Myself RohanSay this: I’m Rohan
Not this: I am staying in AndheriSay this: I live in Andheri
