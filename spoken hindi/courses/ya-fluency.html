Kill the freeze. Proof stories, GDs, aunt-test explanations, meeting one-liners.

Young adults 18–25Intermediate fluency24 lessons24–72 hours depending on timeline

Who this is for

Young adults who understand Hindi films and family talk and still translate every sentence from English before they speak.

They are done when: They can talk for three minutes, tell a proof story, join a GD, and explain their subject in plain Hindi.

Place a student here when: The default course for ‘I understand Hindi but I can’t speak’ at this age.

No mid-sentence correction. Simpler Hindi is a win. Then send them to Interview if they have placements that need Hindi.

Speaking tests

Start90 seconds on a known topic. Time the first pause.
MidIntro, STAR, opinion, GD.
EndShowcase plus a hard question.

01

Samajhta hoon, ab bolo

Admit you already understand, then speak before the English draft arrives.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

मैं समझता हूँ / समझती हूँ, अटक जाता हूँ / जाती हूँ। — I understand; I get stuck.पहले हिंदी में कोशिश। — Hindi first.अंग्रेज़ी बाद में। — English later.एक वाक्य काफी है। — One sentence is enough.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. 90 seconds on ‘mera campus / meri pehli job’. Teacher times the first long pause. No mid-sentence fix.
8–18 min
New language. Rule of the course: silence after a question is allowed for three seconds, then any Hindi noun + hai. Kill the inner translator out loud.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Pair: speaker may not say ‘wait’ in English. Listener only nods or asks क्यों?
30–48 min
Speak freely. Rapid objects: canteen tray, metro card, offer letter — 20 seconds each, no planning.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Voice note with zero script: 40 seconds on today. Send the first take.

Common slips

Not this: Wait, how do I saySay this: एक शब्द बोलो, फिर वाक्य
Not this: Long English draft, then HindiSay this: पहले अधूरा हिंदी वाक्य
02

Campus ya pehli naukri — main kaun

Tell who you are now: course or first job, city, what you actually do all day.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

मैं सिविल में हूँ, तीसरे साल। — I’m in civil, third year.अभी इंटर्न हूँ, रिपोर्टिंग सीख रहा हूँ / रही हूँ। — I’m an intern right now, learning reporting.दिन का बड़ा हिस्सा लैब या एक्सेल में जाता है। — A big part of the day goes in the lab or Excel.घर से बाहर पहला साल है। — It’s the first year away from home.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Cross out cartoon lines: ‘I am a passionate go-getter’.
8–18 min
New language. हूँ / रहा हूँ. One concrete noun per sentence. Excel, lab, intern stay.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Partner writes only the English ghosts they hear, then the speaker retries in Hindi.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Two minutes. If they freeze, point to the board: नाम, जगह, काम, एक सच.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Re-record the intro. Cut any sentence you cannot explain to an aunt.

Common slips

Not this: आई एम अ हार्डवर्किंग स्टूडेंटSay this: मैं सिविल में हूँ, लैब का काम करता / करती हूँ
Not this: मेरा दिन बहुत बिज़ी रहताSay this: दिन का बड़ा हिस्सा लैब में जाता है
03

Proof wali kahani

Tell a proof story: what was on, what you did, what changed — human, not a corporate STAR cartoon.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

एक बार ग्रुप में काम रुक गया था। — Once the group work had stopped.मैंने मीटिंग बुलाई। — I called a meeting.हमने काम बाँटा। — We split the work.सबमिशन टाइम पर हुआ। — The submission happened on time.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Each person names a small real win: fest gate, lab fix, late roommate, a customer.
8–18 min
New language. Shape on the board: कब, मैंने क्या किया, फिर क्या हुआ. The English word STAR only if it helps; never as a chant.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Ninety seconds. Listener asks और तुमने क्या किया? if the story is only ‘we’.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Second round: same story, half the time, still with one I-did line.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Write five Hindi keywords only. Tell the story once to a wall, once to a phone.

Common slips

Not this: सिचुएशन वाज़ चैलेंजिंगSay this: काम रुक गया था
Not this: आई टुक द इनिशिएटिवSay this: मैंने मीटिंग बुलाई
04

Padhai-kaam ki raay

Hold an opinion on study or work for a minute, with one example from this year.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

ऑनलाइन क्लास से मुझे कम समझ आता है। — I understand less from online class.ऑफिस में देर तक बैठना काम नहीं है। — Sitting late in the office is not the work.इंटर्नशिप बिना पे के भी कभी सीख देती है। — An unpaid intern can still teach you, sometimes.लेकिन यह सबके लिए सही नहीं। — But this is not right for everyone.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Board: WFH, attendance, unpaid intern, night-outs before internals. They pick, they speak.
8–18 min
New language. लेकिन / इसके बावजूद / उदाहरण. No essay. One story beat is better than three slogans.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Devil’s pair: you must argue the side you dislike for 45 seconds.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Open chair: 70 seconds, then two questions from the room.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Voice note: opinion, example, other side — under 80 seconds.

Common slips

Not this: आई थिंक ऑनलाइन इज़ बैडSay this: ऑनलाइन क्लास से मुझे कम समझ आता है
Not this: इन कनक्लूज़नSay this: मेरी बात बस इतनी है कि...
05

GD, insaan ki tarah

Join a group discussion like a person: enter, add, disagree, let someone in.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

मैं एक बात जोड़ूँ? — May I add something?आपकी बात सही है, बस एक फर्क है। — You’re right; there’s just one difference.राघव, तुम कुछ कहना चाहते थे। — Raghav, you wanted to say something.समय कम है, निष्कर्ष की तरफ चलें? — Time is short; shall we move to a close?

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Four chairs. Topic they care about: intern stipend, campus politics, city rents.
8–18 min
New language. Teach entry and invite-in. Ban the phrase ‘As I mentioned earlier’ on loop. aap in a formal GD; tum if the room is friends.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Two-minute GD. Teacher only notes who never entered and who ate the air.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Second GD, new topic, roles: quiet one must open, loud one must invite.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Alone, practise the entry line and the invite-in line twice each.

Common slips

Not this: सो बेसिकली आई फीलSay this: मुझे एक बात कहनी है
Not this: यू आर रॉन्गSay this: मैं इससे सहमत नहीं, क्योंकि...
06

Subject chachi ko samjhao

Explain your subject or job to an aunt who is sharp and not in your field.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

चीज़ी बात में, मैं पुलों की मजबूती देखता हूँ / देखती हूँ। — Simply: I look at how strong bridges are.एक्सेल में नंबर नहीं, कहानी देखनी होती है। — In Excel you have to see the story, not just the number.मेरा काम लोगों को फोन पर शांत करना भी है। — My job is also to calm people on the phone.जटिल शब्द बाद में। पहले काम। — Jargon later. Work first.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Teacher is the aunt: ‘Beta yeh MBA / coding / law hai kya?’ They may not hide in English.
8–18 min
New language. चीज़ी बात में / आसान भाषा में. If they cannot do it, they don’t own the subject yet — that’s the point, not shame.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Pair: 90 seconds, then aunt asks क्यों तीन बार.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Hot-seat aunt. Jargon jar: every English technical word costs a Hindi example.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Call or voice-note a family member: your subject in six simple Hindi sentences.

Common slips

Not this: इट्स लाइक अ सॉफ्टवेयर किंड ऑफSay this: मैं एक ऐप ठीक करता / करती हूँ जिससे लोग टिकट काट सकें
Not this: वेरी टेक्निकल है आंटीSay this: आसान भाषा में, मैं ___ करता / करती हूँ

Lab ya office ka phone

Make and take a short work or lab call: name, reason, next step, close.

3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

नमस्ते, मैं अदिति, सिविल लैब से बोल रही हूँ। — Hello, this is Aditi from the civil lab.सैंपल तैयार नहीं आया। — The sample hasn’t come.क्या चार बजे तक हो जाएगा? — Will it be done by four?ठीक है, मैं मेल पर लिख देती हूँ। — Okay, I’ll put it on mail.

18–30 min
Guided practice. Calls: vendor late, faculty not answering, teammate missing the slot.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Bad line / freeze: कहिए, आवाज़ कट रही है, दोबारा बोलिए.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Record one 25-second work call. Listen once. Re-do only the opening.

Common slips

08

Meeting ki ek line

Put one useful sentence into a meeting: update, ask, or block — then stop.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

मेरी तरफ से अपडेट: ड्राफ्ट तैयार है। — Update from me: the draft is ready.मुझे एक क्लैरिटी चाहिए। — I need one clarity.यह शुक्रवार तक नहीं होगा। — This won’t happen by Friday.मैं नोट कर लेता हूँ / लेती हूँ। — I’ll note that.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Stand-up circle. Each person, one sentence, sit. If they pad, they stand again.
8–18 min
New language. Kill office padding: ‘I just wanted to quickly kind of say’. Board the four verbs: तैयार, चाहिए, नहीं होगा, नोट.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Fake meeting of five. Only one useful line each, then a 20-second version if asked.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Chair goes quiet. Someone must ask the date. Practise being that person.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. In a real group chat or meet this week, say one Hindi update out loud or on a voice note.

Common slips

Not this: सो आई जस्ट वांटेड टु सेSay this: मेरी तरफ से अपडेट: ...
Not this: वी विल लुक इंटू इटSay this: मैं शुक्रवार तक चेक करके बताता / बताती हूँ
09

Feedback lo, do

Give and take feedback without disappearing or performing a roast.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

एक चीज़ अच्छी थी: तुम समय पर आए। — One good thing: you came on time.एक चीज़ बदलो: स्लाइड पर लिखकर मत पढ़ो। — Change one thing: don’t read off the slide.यह सुनकर ठीक नहीं लगा, लेकिन समझ गया / गई। — That didn’t feel good to hear, but I got it.मैं अगली बार यह आज़माऊँगा / आज़माऊँगी। — I’ll try this next time.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Last class’s talk in memory. Nobody names a person until they have one glow.
8–18 min
New language. एक अच्छी, एक बदलो. Formal aap if the person is senior. Tum for peers.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Pairs on a 30-second retell. Feedback, then the speaker repeats the grow line.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Hard version: you disagree with the feedback. Stay in Hindi, stay adult.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Send one glow-and-grow voice note about your own last talk.

Common slips

Not this: यू हैव टु बी मोर कॉन्फिडेंटSay this: आवाज़ थोड़ी बड़ी करो, हम तक आनी चाहिए
Not this: ओके ओके सॉरी सॉरीSay this: समझ गया / गई। अगली बार स्लाइड नहीं पढ़ूँगा / पढ़ूँगी

News briefing

3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

दो मिनट में खबर यह है। — In two minutes, the news is this.किसान / स्टूडेंट / ऑफिस — असर उन पर है। — The effect is on them.सरकार कह रही है ___। — The government is saying ___.पक्का नहीं, सोर्स एक है। — Not sure; there’s one source.

18–30 min
Guided practice. Brief a partner who will retell. If the retell dies, the briefing failed.
30–48 min

48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Morning voice note: one story, fact then doubt.

Common slips

Agar main hire karta

Say who you would hire at 21, with proof, not vibes.

6m

Aaj yeh bolo

अगर मैं रखता / रखती, तो उसको रखता / रखती जो काम खत्म करता है। — If I were hiring, I’d take the one who finishes.डिग्री से ज़्यादा मुझे समय पर आना चाहिए। — More than the degree, I need them on time.एक सवाल पूछूँगा: पिछले महीने क्या बनाया? — I’d ask: what did you make last month?मीठी बात से काम नहीं चलता। — Sweet talk doesn’t run the work.

13

Mushkil log

Talk about a difficult person at work or campus without a rant and without becoming sweet-fake.

3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

एक साथी काम टालते हैं। — One teammate keeps putting work off.मैंने साफ़ कहा, यह तेरा हिस्सा है। — I said clearly, this is your part.चिल्लाने से बात नहीं बनी। — Shouting didn’t help.अगली बार मैं लिखकर दूँगा / दूँगी। — Next time I’ll give it in writing.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. No names of people in the room. Invent or blur.
8–18 min
New language. टालना, साफ़ कहा, हिस्सा. Past + next time. Not a therapy session; a speaking task.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Pair: you are the difficult one, then you are the one who asks.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Mediation: three people, five minutes, one spoken agreement.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Rehearse one clean ask you still owe someone, in Hindi, not sent yet.
