Teens · School wali Hindi

Class, tiffin, phone, commute, and a two-minute ordinary day — without nursery talk.

Teens 11–17Beginner34 lessons24–72 hours depending on timeline

Who this is for

Urban teens who hear Hindi at home or on the street but speak very little, or whose only Hindi is a textbook paragraph.

They are done when: The teen can introduce themselves, talk school and weekend, make a phone plan, and repair a rude line (aap vs tum).

Place a student here when: Place here if they freeze after one word, or if their Hindi is only written exam answers.

Respect their age. No animal songs. Use school, phone, and city. Ban ‘Main Ravi hoon’ as the whole identity.

Speaking tests

StartName, class, one like, how they come to school.
Mid (after test 1 lesson)Intro, weekend, shop or phone.
EndTwo-minute day-in-my-life plus polite repair.

01

Naam, class, ek spark

Say your name, class, and one real spark about yourself — not a full identity speech.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

मैं आन्या, आठवीं बी। — I’m Aanya, 8B.मुझे क्रिकेट / डांस / कोडिंग अच्छा लगता है। — I like cricket / dance / coding.तुम्हारा कौन सा सेक्शन है? — Which section are you in?

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Stand. 20-second name chain: name + class only. No long intro. Ban Main Ravi hoon as the whole talk.
8–18 min
New language. Board three slots: नाम, क्लास, एक स्पार्क. Model मैं कबीर, नौवीं ए। मुझे मेट्रो में गाना सुनना पसंद है. Teach लगता है vs हूँ.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Speed pairs: ask सेक्शन and one spark. Swap after 40 seconds. Teacher repairs आप हो if anyone uses aap on a classmate wrongly — classmates get tum.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Mingle of eight: collect four sparks on a scrap. Report one classmate: यह मीरा है, सातवीं सी, उसे स्केच अच्छा लगता है.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. WhatsApp voice note: नाम, क्लास, एक स्पार्क. Stand and speak; do not read.

Common slips

Not this: मैं रवि हूँ। (and then freeze)Say this: मैं रवि, नौवीं ए। मुझे ___ अच्छा लगता है
Not this: आप कौन सी क्लास हो?Say this: तुम कौन सी क्लास में हो? / आप किस क्लास में हैं?
02

Aaj school kaise raha

Tell how school actually went today — tired, okay, or a mess — not only fine.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

आज स्कूल ठीक-ठाक रहा। — School was okay-ish today.बहुत थक गया / थक गई। — I got really tired.पीरियड बढ़िया गया, बट कैंटीन में लाइन थी। — The period went well, but the canteen line was long.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Three corners: बढ़िया / ठीक-ठाक / खराब. Walk to the truth of today and say one word.
8–18 min
New language. Teach रहा / रही for the day, गया / गई for a person. Model नहीं सिर्फ फाइन: आज बोरिंग रहा because double Maths.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Pair interview: आज कौन सा पीरियड भारी था? क्यों? Partner answers in two lines, not one English word.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Hot-seat: teacher is a parent at the gate. Teen reports the day without fine-only. Class votes: did it sound real?
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. At the dinner table say आज स्कूल ___ रहा and one because.

Common slips

Not this: आज स्कूल फाइन था ओनलीSay this: आज स्कूल ठीक-ठाक रहा
Not this: मैं थक गया हैSay this: मैं थक गया हूँ / थक गई हूँ
03

Ghar ke log, asli

Name who actually lives at home and one habit — no baby family song.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

घर में मम्मी, पापा और मैं। — At home: Mum, Dad, and me.मेरी दीदी ट्यूशन जाती है। — My didi goes to tuition.नाना वीकेंड पे आते हैं। — Nana comes on weekends.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Silent count on fingers: how many people sleep in your house tonight. Say the number in Hindi.
8–18 min
New language. Cards: मम्मी, पापा, भाई, दीदी, दादी, नानी, चाचा. Teach रहते हैं / रहती हैं and हैं for elders. Flat / PG / joint — all valid.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Draw a quick house map. Tell a partner who is where at 8 pm: पापा अभी ऑफिस से आ रहे हैं.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Gallery: visit two maps. Ask यह कौन हैं? क्या करते हैं? No ranking whose family is bigger.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Show a photo or empty frame and say three Hindi lines about who is at home.

Common slips

Not this: यह मेरी मम्मी है (for an elder, if the class is ready)Say this: यह मेरी मम्मी हैं
Not this: घर में है पापा मम्मी मैंSay this: घर में पापा, मम्मी और मैं रहते हैं
04

Class mein atka

Survive a stuck moment: I didn’t get it, say it again, I need a leave slip.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

मैम, समझ नहीं आया / आई। — Ma’am, I didn’t get it.एक बार फिर, धीरे। — Once more, slowly.लीव स्लिप के लिए ऑफिस जाना है। — I have to go to the office for a leave slip.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Freeze statues: confused face, raised hand, door knock. Unfreeze only with a Hindi line.
8–18 min
New language. Four lines on the board with aap for the teacher. Gender: आया / आई matches the speaker. Ban tu to the teacher.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Card draw: late, missed homework, didn’t hear the question, need water. Partner is the teacher.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Corridor + office role-play: leave slip, then back to class. Repair any सर तुम.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Use समझ नहीं आया/आई or एक बार फिर once in a real class this week.

Common slips

Not this: सर, तुम दोबारा बोलोSay this: सर, एक बार फिर बताइए
Not this: मैं नहीं समझSay this: समझ नहीं आया / आई
05

Period kab hai

Talk timetable: which period, which room, what is next.

3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

अगला पीरियड केमिस्ट्री है। — Next period is Chemistry.तीसरे पीरियड में लाइब्रेरी है। — Third period is library.टाइमटेबल बदल गया क्या? — Did the timetable change?

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Shout-free: teacher calls a period number; teens say the subject they actually have if they can, or a sample board.
8–18 min
New language. Teach पीरियड, छुट्टी, डबल, सब्स्टिट्यूट. Clock language: कितने बजे, not कितना बजे.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Pairs rebuild a fake Monday on slips. Ask अगला क्या है? कहाँ जाना है?
30–48 min
Speak freely. Chaos bell: substitute teacher. Announce the change standing up in two Hindi sentences.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Voice note: आज मेरे ___ पीरियड में ___ है।

Common slips

Not this: कितना बजे पीरियडSay this: कितने बजे पीरियड है?
Not this: अगला पीरियड केमिस्ट्रीSay this: अगला पीरियड केमिस्ट्री है
06

Tiffin: tez, thanda, bekaar

Give a real tiffin opinion: spicy, cold, good, or skip.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

आज टिफिन ठंडा हो गया। — The tiffin went cold today.थोड़ा तीखा है, मुझे चल जाएगा। — It’s a bit spicy; I can manage.यह मुझे पसंद नहीं, स्वैप करें? — I don’t like this — swap?

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Mystery lunch cards: rajma, idli, maggi, thepla, leftover pizza. Face: yum / okay / no.
8–18 min
New language. Teach तीखा, फीका, ठंडा, गरम, बासी. मुझे ___ पसंद है. Gender: ठंडा / ठंडी with the food they name if it matters today.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Tiffin court: one speaker, one judge. 30 seconds: what’s inside + one opinion + one ask (salt, water, swap).
30–48 min
Speak freely. Canteen vs dabba debate in pairs: आज मैंने ___ लाया/लाई because...
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Open tomorrow’s dabba and send a 15-second voice: तीखा / ठंडा / ठीक.

Common slips

Not this: मैं टिफिन पसंद हैSay this: मुझे यह टिफिन पसंद है
Not this: बहुत स्पाइसी है ओनलीSay this: बहुत तीखा है
07

Meri gali

Describe your gali, society, or block without a tourist speech.

3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

मैं सोसाइटी में रहता हूँ / रहती हूँ। — I live in a society.गली में किराना और एक मंदिर है। — There’s a kirana and a mandir on the street.शाम को बहुत हॉर्न होता है। — In the evening there’s a lot of honking.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. 30-second soundscape: each teen names one sound from home — कुत्ता, मेट्रो, प्रेसर कुकर, लिफ्ट.
8–18 min
New language. के पास, के नीचे, के सामने. Society / chawl / independent house all get the same dignity.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Partner draws from speech only: gate, park, kirana. Then check.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Give a visitor directions from the main road to your building in four short lines.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Walk to your gate and voice-note three things you can see, in Hindi.

Common slips

Not this: मैं सोसाइटी रहताSay this: मैं सोसाइटी में रहता/रहती हूँ
Not this: गली में है किराना ओनलीSay this: गली में किराना है
08

Phone: kahan, late, plan

Make a short phone burst: where are you, I’m late, what’s the plan.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

तुम कहाँ हो? — Where are you?मैं लेट हूँ, मेट्रो में हूँ। — I’m late — I’m on the metro.पांच बजे मॉल के गेट पे मिलते हैं। — Let’s meet at the mall gate at five.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Back-to-back chairs. First 20 seconds: only हेलो and name. No video-call acting.
8–18 min
New language. Teach बोल रहा हूँ / रही हूँ, पहुँच गया / गई, निकल रहा / रही. Friends = tum. Parent = aap if that is their home rule; many urban homes use tum with mum — let them choose and stay consistent.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Three missed-call cards: tuition late, forgot tiffin, plan change. Pair calls.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Group of three: one is busy parent, one is friend, one is calling. Register must shift.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Send the teacher a 20-second fake missed-call voice: कहाँ हो + प्लान.

Common slips

Not this: मैं लेट हैSay this: मैं लेट हूँ
Not this: मिलते हैं हम मॉलSay this: हम मॉल के गेट पे मिलते हैं
09

Asli shauk

Talk a real hobby — reels, football, sketch, coding — not stamp collecting unless it’s true.

3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

मेरा शौक फुटबॉल है। — My hobby is football.मैं हर शाम आधा घंटा स्केच करती हूँ / करता हूँ। — I sketch for half an hour every evening.मुझे शॉर्ट्स बनाना अच्छा लगता है। — I like making shorts.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Two truths: write a fake hobby and a real one. Partner guesses.
8–18 min
New language. शौक, अच्छा लगता है, सीख रहा/रही हूँ. Ban the textbook stamp-collection sentence unless someone actually does it.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Speed dating chairs: 45 seconds each — what, when, why one line.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Show-and-tell without the object if needed: phone photo of a sketch, a match, a playlist name.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Voice note: मेरा असली शौक ___ है, क्योंकि ___.

Common slips

Not this: मेरा हॉबी है क्रिकेट ओनलीSay this: मेरा शौक क्रिकेट है / मुझे क्रिकेट अच्छा लगता है
Not this: मैं करता स्केच शामSay this: मैं शाम को स्केच करता/करती हूँ
10

Canteen ke paise

Order at the canteen and talk money without freezing on numbers.

3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

एक समोसा और चाय देना। — One samosa and chai, please.यह कितने का है? — How much is this?मेरे पास सिर्फ पचास हैं। — I only have fifty.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Paper notes 10/20/50. Quick कीमत shout for samosa, bun-maska, nimbu.
8–18 min
New language. कितने का / कितनी की. देना to the canteen uncle/aunty with aap if they don’t know them. Change: वापस ___ दीजिए.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Pair shop: order two things, add, pay, take change. If short, बचे हुए पैसे कल दूँगा/दूँगी.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Rush hour: four customers, one counter. Polite, loud enough, no cutting the line in English only.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. If you buy something this week, say यह कितने का है? yourself.

Common slips

Not this: यह कितना है समोसाSay this: यह समोसा कितने का है?
Not this: पैसे दो वापस तुमSay this: वापस पैसे दीजिए
11

Rasta: metro, bus, auto

Ask and give a city route: metro, bus, or auto.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

यह मेट्रो कहाँ जाएगी? — Where will this metro go?ऑटोवाले, राजौरी तक कितना लोगे? — Auto-wale, how much to Rajouri?सीधा जाकर बाएँ मुड़ना। — Go straight and turn left.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Floor tape: platform, bus stop, auto stand. Teens stand on a spot and name it.
8–18 min
New language. सीधा, बाएँ, दाएँ, अगला स्टेशन, उतरना. Aap with the stranger; tum only if they role-play a friend guiding.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Pairs: I am new, I need the mall / tuition / station. Partner gives three steps.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Lost-on-Yellow-Line: one phone map (paper). Ask two people, then an auto. End at the gate.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Voice note: घर से स्कूल — मेट्रो / बस / ऑटो में दो लाइनें.

Common slips

Not this: यहाँ से मॉल कैसेSay this: यहाँ से मॉल कैसे जाएँ?
Not this: लेफ्ट लेनाSay this: बाएँ मुड़ना / बाएँ लेना
12

Pressure, bina drama

Name exam, fight, or crush-level stress in age-safe Hindi — no gossip show.

3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

मुझे बहुत प्रेशर लग रहा है। — I’m feeling a lot of pressure.कल टेस्ट है, नींद नहीं आई। — There’s a test tomorrow; I couldn’t sleep.दोस्तों में थोड़ी लड़ाई हो गई। — There was a bit of a fight among friends.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Anonymous slip: one pressure word. Teacher reads a few aloud; class only says मुझे भी if true.
8–18 min
New language. लग रहा है, टेंशन, घबराहट. Crush = किसी को लेकर घबराहट or मन नहीं लग रहा — no names, no teasing. Fight stays light: बात बिगड़ गई.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Partner: 40 seconds of truth + one coping line: मैं चलूँगा/चलूँगी, पानी पिया, मम्मी से कहा.
30–48 min
Speak freely. Advice hotline: listener only says समझ सकती/सकता हूँ and one kind line. No fixing lives.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Private voice note to the teacher or a parent: एक असली प्रेशर, एक लाइन.

Common slips

Not this: मैं प्रेशर हूँSay this: मुझे प्रेशर लग रहा है
Not this: मैं स्लीप नहीं कियाSay this: मुझे नींद नहीं आई
13

Pichhla weekend

Tell last weekend as it happened — sleep, tuition, relatives, phone — not a picnic essay.

6w3m6m

Aaj yeh bolo

शनिवार को मैं सोता रहा / सोती रही। — On Saturday I just kept sleeping.रविवार को नानी के घर गए। — On Sunday we went to Nani’s.थोड़ा होमवर्क भी किया। — I also did a bit of homework.

Bring Board, a few pictures or real objects, and space for pair work.

0–8 min
Warm-up. Timeline on the board: Sat morning / Sat night / Sunday. Teens tick what was true.
8–18 min
New language. Past: गया / गई, किया / की, देखा / देखी. Don’t teach the whole tense table. कल = that weekend from context.
18–30 min
Guided practice. Partner interview: तीन सवाल — कहाँ गए, किसके साथ, बोर हुआ/हुई क्या?
30–48 min
Speak freely. 2-minute story in threes. If nothing happened, that is the story: घर पे था/थी, फोन चलाया.
48–55 min
Polish. Invite two or three students to speak. Correct only the target sentences. End with a class repeat.
55–60 min
Homework. Voice note: पिछला शनिवार, तीन वाक्य, आँखें खुली।

Common slips
